# Approvals

The **Approvals** page collects everything waiting for a decision: sales orders, purchase orders, new customers and payment deductions. The sidebar shows how many are waiting.

![The Approvals page]({{ '/assets/images/selling/appr-01-list.webp' | relative_url }})

## Approve a Sales Order

Open the order from Approvals and choose:

- **Approve** — the app checks the customer's credit and your stock, takes the stock, and **makes the invoice automatically**.
- **Return for Changes** — back to Draft with your note, so the writer can fix it.
- **Reject / Cancel** — the order is stopped.

Approval is refused if the customer is **on credit hold** or there isn't enough stock. When two orders want the same last items, the first one approved gets them.

## Hear New Approvals

When something new arrives, approvers hear a soft chime and see "● New Approval" in the browser tab. On a computer you can turn on **My Account → Desktop Notifications** to get the Mac or Windows alert sound instead.

{: .note }
Not hearing anything? See **My Account → Notifications → "Not hearing new approvals?"** for the steps for your browser.

## Who Can Approve

Admins approve everything. Staff can approve sales orders only if the admin gives them permission to approve orders in **Settings → Users & Permissions**.
