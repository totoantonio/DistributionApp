# Invoices and Delivery

## The Invoice

Every approved order gets an invoice (`INV-00001`) automatically. Open it from **Invoices** to see the items, payments, delivery steps and the printable Sales Invoice.

{: .important }
Payment terms count **from delivery**. Until the goods are delivered, the invoice shows "After Delivery" and can't become overdue.

## Pack and Ship

Delivery moves one step at a time on the invoice page:

**Pending → Packed → For Delivery → Delivered**

Warehouse staff click the next step as they go. The **Invoices** count in the sidebar shows how many are waiting to be packed.

![The delivery steps at the top of an invoice]({{ '/assets/images/selling/inv-02-steps.webp' | relative_url }})

## The Delivery Page

Drivers use the **Delivery** page in the sidebar. It lists every invoice ready to go, with its delivery instructions and a green round **Mark Delivered** button (✓). Marking an invoice delivered sets its due date: the delivery date plus the customer's terms.

![The Delivery page]({{ '/assets/images/selling/inv-03-delivery.webp' | relative_url }})

A **Delivery** login sees only this page and nothing else in the app.

## Print the Invoice or Delivery Receipt

On the invoice page, switch between **Sales Invoice** and **Delivery Receipt**, then click the printer. Both print on A4 with your company details. Drivers can open the Delivery Receipt from their Delivery page.

## Signed Delivery Receipts (Add-on)

With this add-on turned on in **Settings → Features**, the Delivery page lists recent deliveries, each with a paperclip. Take a photo of the signed receipt (or choose a file), and it's kept with the invoice.

## Editing an Invoice

Notes can always change. The date and terms lock once the invoice is delivered or has a payment — after that only the admin can change them, with a reason.
