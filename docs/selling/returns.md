# Returns and Cancelling

## Goods Sent Back — Credit Memo

On a **delivered** invoice, click **Create Credit Memo**. Type how many of each item came back and the reason, then save.

The app:

- puts the stock back,
- lowers the invoice (and the customer's balance, sales and the salesman's figures),
- gives the credit memo its own number (`CM-00001`) and a printable form.

If the customer already paid more than the new total, the invoice shows a **customer credit**.

See all credit memos in **Reports → Sales → Credit Memos**.

## Cancel an Invoice

Before delivery, an invoice with **no payments** can be cancelled (with permission to cancel orders; reason required). The order is cancelled too and the stock goes back.

After delivery, only the admin can **void** an invoice, and only if it has no payments.

{: .note }
To change what a customer receives **before** delivery, edit the order instead — or cancel and make a new one.
