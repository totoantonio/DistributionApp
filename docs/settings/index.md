# Settings

**Settings** (admin only) is in the sidebar under Admin. Pick a section on the left:

| Section | What It's For |
|---|---|
| **Company Profile** | Name, logo, address, TIN, bank details and invoice fine print — printed on your forms |
| **Appearance** | App font; show or hide the demo bar |
| **Features** | Turn optional features on or off — see [Features and Add-ons]({{ '/settings/features/' | relative_url }}) |
| **Document Numbering** | Prefixes for numbers like `SO-`, `INV-`, `CUST-` (new records only) |
| **Users & Permissions** | See [Users and Permissions]({{ '/settings/users/' | relative_url }}) |
| **Backup & Restore** | See [Backups and Updates]({{ '/settings/backups-updates/' | relative_url }}) |
| **Start Up** | Start the app when the computer starts — see [Keep It Running]({{ '/getting-started/keep-running/' | relative_url }}) |
| **Remote Access** | Reach the app outside the office with Tailscale |
| **About** | Version, updates and license |
| **Legal & Regulatory** | License agreement, warranty, privacy |
| **Danger Zone** | Delete the company and start over |
| **Quit** | Stop the app (a backup is saved) |

![The Settings menu]({{ '/assets/images/settings/set-01-overview.png' | relative_url }})

Everyone, including staff, also has **[My Account]({{ '/settings/my-account/' | relative_url }})** under their picture.

{: .warning }
**Danger Zone → Delete Company** erases everything. It asks for the company name and your password, and saves a backup first — but treat it as final.
