# Users and Permissions

## Roles

| Role | Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Everything, including Settings, approving customers, payroll and removing records |
| **Staff** | Only what the admin ticks for them |
| **Salesman** | Their own customers, orders and performance — no costs, suppliers or company-wide reports |
| **Delivery** | Only the Delivery page: Mark Delivered and the Delivery Receipt |

## Add a User

**Settings → Users & Permissions** → add a user: username, password, role. For staff, tick what they may do, grouped by area (sales, payments, stock, reports…). Hover over a permission to see what it allows.

![The permission ticks for a staff user]({{ '/assets/images/settings/usr-01-add.webp' | relative_url }})

Your plan sets how many users can be active: **Startup 3, Enterprise 10, Superuser 20**.

## Start Page

Open a user to choose the page they see after signing in — e.g. Stock Arrivals for the warehouse.

## Review Permissions

**Review Permissions** shows every permission against every user in one table. Click a box to give or take a permission.

## Someone Leaves

**Deactivate** the user — they're signed out everywhere and can't sign in. Their past work stays in the records and the Activity Log.

{: .note }
Every change in the app is kept in the **Activity Log** (Admin → Activity Log): who, when, from which device, and what changed. It can't be edited or deleted.
