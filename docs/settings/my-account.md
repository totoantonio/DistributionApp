# My Account

Everyone has **My Account** — click your picture at the top right.

## Change Your Password

Type your current password, then the new one twice. You'll be signed out on your other devices.

## Appearance and Accessibility

Saved for **you**, on every device you use:

- **Light, Dark or Automatic**
- **Increase Contrast**, **Bold Text**, **Button Shapes**
- **Reduce Motion**, **Reduce Transparency**
- **Display Zoom** — Default, Larger or Largest

## Notifications

- **Desktop Notifications** (computers) — approvals come with the Mac or Windows alert sound.
- **Lock-Screen Notifications** (salesmen's phones) — needs the app on the Home Screen over a secure address.

## Lock or Log Out

From the same menu: **Lock Screen** when you step away (sign in again to return), or **Log Out**.
