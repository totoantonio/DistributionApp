# Features and Add-ons

**Settings → Features** has a switch for each optional feature.

| Feature | What It Does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Privacy Screen** | Blurs the page after 1–10 idle minutes; any touch or key brings it back | On |
| **Require Proof of Order** | A salesman's order stays a Draft until a signed PO or photo is attached | Off |
| **Payment QR Code** *(add-on)* | Your GCash / QR Ph code on a printable "Scan to Pay" sheet | Off |
| **Salesman Locations** *(add-on, Beta)* | Check-ins at customers, where orders were taken, and asking a salesman where they are — only after they agree | Off |
| **Signed Delivery Receipts** *(add-on)* | Drivers photograph the signed receipt on the Delivery page | Off |

![The switches in Settings → Features]({{ '/assets/images/settings/feat-01-switches.png' | relative_url }})

## Add-ons

Add-ons are paid extras. They're included in your **activation code** — buying one later means a new code, pasted in **License** the same way. During the free trial and in the demo, every add-on is on so you can try it.

## Payment QR Code

After turning it on, upload your QR in **Company Profile** with the account name and number. Everyone can then open **Downloads → Payment QR Code** to print it or save it as a picture for customers.

## Salesman Locations

Each salesman is asked once whether to share their location, and can turn it off in **My Account**. Location is taken only while the app is open on their phone — never in the background — and only when they save an order or payment, check in, or answer your request.

{: .note }
Phones share location only over a secure (https) address — turn on **Remote Access** first.
