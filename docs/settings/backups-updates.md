# Backups and Updates

## Automatic Backups

The app saves a backup **every day** and **every time it quits**, and keeps the last 14. You can also click **Back Up Now** in **Settings → Backup & Restore**.

![The backup list in Settings → Backup & Restore]({{ '/assets/images/settings/bak-01-list.png' | relative_url }})

## Keep a Copy Somewhere Else

A backup on the same computer won't help if that computer is lost. Under **Also Copy Backups To**, pick a USB drive or your Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox folder. Every backup is then copied there too.

{: .important }
If the copy fails (e.g. the USB drive is unplugged), Settings shows an orange note. Your backups on the computer are still made.

## Restore a Backup

Pick a backup → **Restore**. The app checks the file first, restarts, and comes back with that day's data. Your current data is kept aside until the restore is complete.

## Updates

The app checks for a new version once a day. Admins see **Update Available** at the top.

**Settings → About → Install Update**. The app downloads the new version, checks it's genuine, saves a backup, replaces itself and restarts. Your data isn't touched.

{: .note }
With a one-time purchase, updates are included until the date on your license. After that the app keeps working on your version; **Renew** to get newer ones.

## Your License

Click your picture → **License** to see your plan, add-ons, user limit and updates date, or to paste a new activation code.
