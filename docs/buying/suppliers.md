# Suppliers

**Suppliers** lists everyone you buy from. Open one to see or edit:

- **Terms** (days to pay) and the **due date basis** — counted from their invoice date or from the day the goods arrived
- **Lead time** — days from ordering to arrival (used by suggested purchase orders)
- **Contact person**, numbers, email and address
- **Bank or e-wallet** details for paying them
- **Brands carried** and recent arrivals

The **Supplier Ledger** button shows every bill, short delivery and payment with the running balance — what you owe them.

{: .note }
Bank details show only to people who can pay suppliers.
