# Pay Suppliers

Every received stock arrival is a bill. Its due date is the supplier's invoice date (or the arrival date, depending on the supplier) plus their terms.

## Pay a Bill

On the arrival, use **Pay Supplier**: date, amount, method and reference no. The supplier's bank details are shown so you know where to send it.

![The Pay Supplier card on a stock arrival]({{ '/assets/images/buying/pay-01-supplier.webp' | relative_url }})

## Issue a Post-Dated Check

Choose method **Check** with a later date — the check is **pending** until you **Mark Cleared** on the Checks page. The bill shows "Check Issued" and isn't overdue meanwhile.

## What You Owe

**Reports → Accounts Payable**:

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All Payables** | Every unpaid bill with its due date |
| **Due This Month** | What to pay this month, including overdue |
| **By Supplier** | Aging per supplier |
| **Checks Issued** | Your checks by date — pending, due today, cleared |
| **BIR Taxes** | Estimated VAT or percentage tax per quarter |
