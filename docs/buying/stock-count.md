# Stock Count

Count your shelves and let the app fix the differences.

1. **Products** → the clipboard icon (**Stock Count**).
2. Filter by supplier or search to find products. Print the count sheet if you count on paper.
3. Type what you counted — cases and pieces. The difference shows as you type. Leave a row blank to skip it.
4. Add a note and **Save Count**.

![A few rows of the Stock Count page]({{ '/assets/images/buying/cnt-01-count.png' | relative_url }})

Each changed product gets a stock adjustment (reason "Count Correction") in its history.

{: .note }
You need permission to adjust stock. For a single product, use **Adjust Stock** on the product instead.

## Inventory Reports

**Reports → Inventory**: stock on hand and its value, and **Days Covered** — how many days your stock will last at the current sales rate (Critical under 7 days, Low under 14).
