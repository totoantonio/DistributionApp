# Collecting

This chapter covers getting paid and keeping track of who still owes you.

1. **[Receive a Payment]({{ '/collecting/payments/' | relative_url }})** — cash, bank transfer, GCash, Maya or check, over one or several invoices.
2. **[Deductions]({{ '/collecting/deductions/' | relative_url }})** — when a customer pays less because of withholding tax, wrong pricing or support.
3. **[Post-Dated Checks]({{ '/collecting/checks/' | relative_url }})** — hold them until they clear.
4. **[Customer Deposits]({{ '/collecting/deposits/' | relative_url }})** — money received before there's an invoice.
5. **[Who Owes What]({{ '/collecting/statements/' | relative_url }})** — statements, ledgers and receivables reports.

{: .important }
Payment terms count **from delivery**. An invoice isn't due — and can't be overdue — until it's marked Delivered.
