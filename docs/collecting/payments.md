# Receive a Payment

## One Payment, Several Invoices

Go to **Payments** → **Receive Payment**.

1. Pick the **customer** (or type an invoice number). Their open invoices appear, oldest first.
2. Type the **amount received**. It's spread over the invoices, oldest first. Change the split if the customer asks.
3. Choose the **method** — Cash, Bank Transfer, GCash, Maya, MariBank, Atome, Check or Other — and type the **reference no.** (OR, transfer or check number). The reference is required.
4. Click **Save Payment**.

![The Receive Payment page]({{ '/assets/images/collecting/pay-01-receive.png' | relative_url }})

The payment gets its own number (`PAY-00001`). An invoice that isn't fully paid shows **Partial** with what's still owed.

You can attach one photo or PDF to the payment, such as a deposit slip or a BIR Form 2307.

## Pay One Invoice

On an invoice page you can also record a payment for just that invoice. A payment can never be more than the balance.

## The Acknowledgment Receipt

Each payment has a printable **Acknowledgment of Payment** (A4): who paid, the amount in words, the invoices it covered and who received it.

{: .warning }
This is **not an Official Receipt**. An Official or Collection Receipt must be a BIR-registered form — keep issuing yours as usual.

## Removing a Payment

Only the admin can remove a payment, with a reason. It's kept in the Activity Log.
