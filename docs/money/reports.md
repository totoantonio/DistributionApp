# Reports

**Reports** opens with four headline cards — Sales, Receivables, Payables and Inventory Health — and every other report grouped below.

![The four headline cards on the Reports page]({{ '/assets/images/money/rep-01-hub.png' | relative_url }})

Every report has date shortcuts, filters that apply as you change them, **Print / Save PDF** and **Export CSV**.

## The Main Ones

| Group | Reports |
|---|---|
| **Financial** (admin) | Income Statement, Cash & Collections, General Ledger, Trial Balance, Balance Sheet |
| **Sales** | Sales Analysis, Sales Summary, Invoice Summary, Customer Sales, Accounts Receivable, Collection Forecast, Statement of Account, Credit Memos, Customer Deductions |
| **Payables** | Accounts Payable, BIR Taxes |
| **Inventory** | Inventory Report, Days Covered |
| **Accounting & Tax** | Taxes Invoiced |
| **Expenses** | Expense Summary |
| **Team** | Salesman Performance, User Summary |

## Sales Analysis

Group sales by supplier, brand, product, part number, customer or day — with gross profit for people allowed to see it.

## Salesman Performance

Each salesman sees **My Performance**: their monthly sales chart, sales by brand and their expenses. The admin can pick any salesman and also see gross profit, costs and the profit they made for the company.

## Who Sees Which Report

The admin picks, per person, which report cards they see: **Settings → Users & Permissions**.
