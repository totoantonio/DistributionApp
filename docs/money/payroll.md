# Payroll

**Payroll** (admin only) works out your people's pay twice a month: the **1st–15th** and the **16th–end of month**.

## 1. Add Employees

**Payroll → Add Employee**: name, position, contact and emergency contact, monthly salary or daily rate, TIN, SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG numbers, and a photo. You can also give them an app login.

## 2. Prepare a Payroll

**Prepare** the cut-off. The app works out each person's pay and deductions: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and withholding tax.

Type any **absences**, then **Recompute**.

![One payroll run with its totals]({{ '/assets/images/money/pr-02-run.webp' | relative_url }})

## 3. Post It

**Post** locks the payroll and records the salaries and employer contributions as expenses. Print the **payslips** — one A4 page per employee.

Found a mistake after posting? **Reopen for Editing** (reason required) removes those expenses and returns it to Draft.

## Government Rates

The SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR tables can be updated when the government changes them: **Payroll → Government Rates → Edit**. Each change starts on the date you choose and needs a source note. The page links to the official sites.
