# Books and BIR

## Your Books, Kept for You

You don't post journal entries. The app builds the books from your invoices, payments, stock arrivals, supplier payments and expenses — so they always match.

**Reports → Financial Statements** (admin):

- **General Journal** — every entry
- **General Ledger** — all accounts or one, with running balances. **Export CSV** gives your bookkeeper the whole ledger.
- **Trial Balance**
- **Balance Sheet**
- **Income Statement**

## BIR Taxes

**Reports → Accounts Payable → BIR Taxes** estimates the tax on what you **collected**, per quarter:

- **VAT-registered** — 12/112 output VAT (Form 2550Q)
- **Non-VAT** — 3% percentage tax (Form 2551Q)

Each quarter's tax is due on the 25th day after the quarter ends, and it shows in Accounts Payable until it's paid.

## Record a BIR Payment

Open **BIR Payments** from the BIR Taxes view → **Record Payment**: date, amount, method and reference. The quarter's balance drops, and so does your money on hand.

{: .warning }
Input VAT on your purchases isn't deducted yet, and this app is **not a BIR-registered accounting system (CAS)**. Ask your accountant or RDO before relying on it for filing.
