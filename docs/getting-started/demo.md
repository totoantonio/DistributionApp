# Try the Demo

Not ready to enter your own company details yet? Explore a complete sample business first — customers, products, orders in every stage, payments and reports.

## Start the Demo

On the **Welcome** screen, click **Explore**.

![The Welcome screen, with Explore outlined]({{ '/assets/images/getting-started/demo-00-welcome.webp' | relative_url }})

The app fills itself with **Demo Auto Parts**, a made-up distributor in Quezon City, and shows the sign-in screen.

![The sign-in screen]({{ '/assets/images/getting-started/demo-01-login.webp' | relative_url }})

## Demo Logins

Sign in as different people to see what each one sees. The password is **demo123** for all of them. The sign-in screen lists them too.

| Username | Who | What They See |
|---|---|---|
| **demo** | Admin (owner) | Everything |
| **staff** | Office staff | Customers, orders, invoices, payments |
| **sales** | Salesman | Only their own customers and orders |
| **warehouse** | Warehouse staff | Packing, stock arrivals, stock adjustments |
| **driver** | Delivery | Only the Delivery page |

The dark bar at the top reminds you that you're in the demo and lists these logins.

![The demo bar at the top of the page]({{ '/assets/images/getting-started/demo-02-demo-bar.webp' | relative_url }})

## Leave the Demo

In the dark bar, click **Exit Demo** to go back to the Welcome screen, or **Exit & Set Up My Company** to go straight to [setting up your real company]({{ '/getting-started/first-setup/' | relative_url }}). Either way, all sample data is removed.

{: .note }
Nothing you do in the demo is kept, so try anything — approve orders, record payments, print invoices.
