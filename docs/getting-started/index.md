# Getting Started

Distribution App runs on **one main computer** in your office. Everyone else — staff, salesmen and drivers — opens it in the web browser on their own phone or computer. There's nothing to install on their devices.

Follow these pages in order:

1. **Install the app** on your main computer — [Mac]({{ '/getting-started/install-mac/' | relative_url }}) or [Windows]({{ '/getting-started/install-windows/' | relative_url }}).
2. **[Try the Demo]({{ '/getting-started/demo/' | relative_url }})** — explore with a sample company first (optional).
3. **[Set Up Your Company]({{ '/getting-started/first-setup/' | relative_url }})** — your admin account and business details.
4. **[Connect Your Staff]({{ '/getting-started/staff-access/' | relative_url }})** — phones and computers in the office, and outside.
5. **[Keep It Running]({{ '/getting-started/keep-running/' | relative_url }})** — so the app is always there when staff need it.

{: .note }
Check the [System Requirements](https://github.com/totoantonio/DistributionApp#system-requirements) before you start: macOS 13 or later, or Windows 10/11 (64-bit).
