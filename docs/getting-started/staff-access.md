# Connect Your Staff

Staff, salesmen and drivers use the app in their phone's or computer's web browser. You give each person a login, and they open the app's address.

## 1. Add Each Person

Go to **Settings → Users & Permissions** and add a user for each person, with the role that fits: **Staff**, **Salesman** or **Delivery**. Tick what each staff member may do. (A full page on users and permissions is coming in the Settings chapter.)

## 2. Give Them the Address

Open **Staff Access** (bottom of the sidebar). It shows the address to open — with a QR code, **Copy Address** and **Share**.

![The Staff Access page]({{ '/assets/images/getting-started/staff-01-access.webp' | relative_url }})

- **In the Office** — works on the same Wi-Fi or office network. No internet needed. Under the address is a second one ending in **.local** — it keeps working even when the router changes the number.
- **Outside the Office** — appears after you turn on **Settings → Remote Access** (uses a free Tailscale account). Salesmen can then use the app on mobile data.

## 3. Open It on a Phone

On the phone, open the camera and point it at the QR code, then tap the link.

Sign in with the username and password you gave them.

## 4. Add It to the Home Screen (iPhone)

In Safari, tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**. The app then opens full screen from its own icon, like any other app.

![Share → Add to Home Screen]({{ '/assets/images/getting-started/phone-02-add-home-screen.png' | relative_url }})

On Android, open the browser menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen**.

{: .note }
The main computer must be on and the app running for staff to connect — see [Keep It Running]({{ '/getting-started/keep-running/' | relative_url }}).
