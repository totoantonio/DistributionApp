# Set Up Your Company

It takes about 5 minutes. Have your business registration details and TIN ready.

## 1. Start Setup

On the **Welcome** screen, click **Set Up Company**. One page opens with two parts: **Admin Account** and **Company Profile**.

![The Welcome screen — Set Up Company]({{ '/assets/images/getting-started/setup-01-welcome.png' | relative_url }})

## 2. Create Your Admin Account

This is **your** login as the owner — it can do everything, including adding staff later.

- **Username** — for example your first name.
- **Password** — at least 6 characters. Type it twice.

![Admin account]({{ '/assets/images/getting-started/setup-03-admin.png' | relative_url }})

{: .important }
Write the password down somewhere safe. Only an admin can add or reset other users.

## 3. Enter Your Company Profile

These details print on your invoices, sales orders and statements. Fields marked **\*** are required:

- **Registered business name**, and your **trade name** if you use one
- **City / municipality** and **Province** (plus street, barangay and ZIP code)
- **Contact number(s)**
- **TIN** and **VAT status** (VAT-registered or Non-VAT)

Optional: **company logo**, a **short name** and **tagline** (shown in the sidebar), email, website, Facebook page, and your **bank details** (printed under "Payment Details" so customers know where to pay).

![Company profile]({{ '/assets/images/getting-started/setup-02-company.png' | relative_url }})

## 4. Finish

Click **Finish Setup**. You're signed in and the app opens.

![The app after setup]({{ '/assets/images/getting-started/app-01-first-page.png' | relative_url }})

You can change any of this later in **Settings → Company Profile**.

## Free Trial and Activation

The app is free to use for **15 days**, with every feature on. After that it turns **read-only**: you can still view, print and export, but not make changes.

To keep working, buy a plan and you'll receive an **activation code**. Click your picture (top right) → **License**, paste the code in **Activation Code**, and click **Activate**. Your data stays as it is.

{: .important }
The code is made for your company name. If you rename the company later, ask for a new code.
