# The Dashboard and Sidebar

## The Dashboard

Admins and staff open on the **Dashboard** after signing in. Tap any card or row to open it.

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Sales This Month** | Sales so far this month, and the change from the same days last month |
| **Collections This Month** | Money received this month (admins), and the change from last month |
| **Overdue** | How much is overdue and on how many invoices — opens the overdue invoices |
| **Waiting for Approval** | How many orders, customers or deductions need your decision |
| **This Week** | Sales per day, Sunday to Saturday |
| **Orders for Approval** / **Invoices to Pack** | The oldest five of each |
| **Reminders** | Checks due by tomorrow and products at or below their reorder level |

Each person sees only the parts they are allowed to open. Salesmen open on **My Performance** and drivers on **Delivery**. To open a person on another page, see [Start Page]({{ '/settings/users/' | relative_url }}).

## The Sidebar

- **Hide or show it** with the button at the top left of the page, or press **Control‑Command‑S** on a Mac (**Control‑Alt‑S** on Windows).
- **Fold a section** — click **Sales**, **Inventory** or **Money**. The section with the page you're on stays open.
- **Your name** at the bottom opens **My Account**; the **gear** beside it opens **Settings** (admins).
- **Staff Access**, **Activity Log** and **Log Out** are in your picture menu at the top right.

The app remembers your choices and keeps them on every computer you sign in on.
