## From Order to Collection — In One Place

Your salesman takes the order on their phone. You approve it with one tap — the invoice is created and the stock is taken out for you. The warehouse packs it, the driver marks it delivered, and the payment terms start counting by themselves. When the customer pays — cash, bank, GCash, Maya, or a post-dated check — you record it, and your reports, receivables, and books are already up to date.

**No spreadsheets. No copying. No "who has the latest file?"**

## Who It's For

Distributors and wholesalers with **2 to 20 people** — an owner, a few salesmen, someone in the office, a warehouse, a driver. Auto parts, lubricants, hardware, grocery and FMCG, pharma, school and office supplies — if you sell to stores on credit and deliver, it's made for you.

## Add-ons and Options

Everything above works on your office Wi-Fi, with **no internet at all**. These are extras for businesses that want them — switch them on in **Settings → Features**. All are **included free in the 15-day trial**, so you can try them before you decide.

- **Work From Anywhere** <sup>Optional</sup> — Let staff use the app outside the office too: salesmen on the road, you at home. Turn on **Remote Access** with one button (it uses Tailscale, free for small teams); the app stays private and is never open to the public internet. It needs internet on both ends — but if the connection drops, **the office keeps working**, and salesmen's phones keep new orders and send them once they're back online.

- **Salesman Locations (Visits)** <sup>Beta</sup> — Uses the salesmen's phones over Work From Anywhere. Salesmen **Check In** at each customer, and the **Visits** page shows **where each sales order and payment was made and how far from the store**. Need to know where someone is? **Ping** them — with an optional message like *"Pls proceed to Tondo Auto Supply"* — and a pop-up on their phone lets them share their location with one tap. Each salesman agrees once, nothing is tracked in the background, and every request is logged — made with the Data Privacy Act in mind.
- **Payment QR Code** <sup>Beta</sup> — Upload your GCash or QR Ph code once. Every salesman can show it on their phone or send it as an image or PDF, with a clear reminder printed on it: **salesmen don't accept cash** — checks or QR only, and always ask for an official receipt.
- **Signed Delivery Receipts** — Drivers snap a photo of the signed delivery receipt (or attach a PDF) right on the Delivery page, so you have proof of every delivery, kept with the invoice.

**Each add-on is paid for separately** — Payment QR Code, Salesman Locations, and Signed Delivery Receipts — so you pay only for the ones you use. They are unlocked with your activation code — **ask us for pricing**. Work From Anywhere is included.

## Questions Owners Ask

**What if the office computer breaks?**
A backup is saved every day and every time the app quits, and each backup can also be copied by itself to a USB drive or your Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox folder — restoring it on a new computer brings everything back.

**Do I need internet?**
No. Staff in the office connect over your Wi-Fi. Internet is only needed for staff outside the office (through Remote Access), for update checks, and for activation.

**Can I see everything from my phone?**
Yes — on your office Wi-Fi, open it in your phone's browser and add it to your Home Screen; it opens like an app. Want it outside the office too? Turn on **Work From Anywhere**.

**What if I stop subscribing?**
Your data stays on your computer. The app turns read-only, so you can still open and print everything, and subscribing again picks up right where you left off.

**Is it BIR-accredited?**
No. It is not a registered Computerized Accounting System. Tax figures are estimates to help you prepare — please have your accountant check them before filing. Ask your RDO what applies to your business.

**Can I try it first?**
Yes — a free 15-day trial with up to 20 users and every add-on, plus a **Demo Mode** filled with sample customers, products, and orders so you can explore without typing anything. Everything in the demo is made up — no real stores, people, or prices.

**How do I move from my old system or spreadsheets?**
Import your price lists from PDF, Excel, or CSV in minutes, and add customers by uploading their filled-in information sheet. Optional setup help is available — we'll do it with you.
